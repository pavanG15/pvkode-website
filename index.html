Welcome to PVKode Works. We develop simple, user-friendly Android applications and games, including but not limited to Diwali Arcade Game Hub, Hanuman Jyotish, and other utility tools.

Your privacy is strictly respected and protected. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle data across all applications published by PVKode Works on the Google Play Store.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal sensitive user data (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) through our applications.

Local Device Data

For our games (like Diwali Arcade Game Hub), we use your device's local storage to save non-personal application data such as:

  • In-game High Scores and level progress.
  • User preferences (e.g., Language selection, Sound/Mute settings).

This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (AdMob)

To keep our applications free, we use third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdMob. These third-party services may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve appropriate ads and measure their performance.
  • Device Information: Third-party SDKs may collect non-personal information such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, and network status to optimize ad delivery.

By using our apps, you consent to the data collection practices of these third-party providers. You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy Policy.

Users can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting their Android device Settings -> Google -> Ads -> Opt out of Ads Personalization.

3. App Permissions Explained

Our applications may request certain standard device permissions to function correctly:

  • INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE: Required to display advertisements via Google AdMob and to load web-based game assets.
  • VIBRATE: Used in our games to provide haptic feedback (e.g., when a firecracker bursts or a button is clicked) for a better user experience.

We only request permissions that are absolutely necessary for the app to function.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications are built for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
